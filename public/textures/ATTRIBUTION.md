# Planetary Texture Attributions & Scientific Sources
## PJMC STUDENT — SOLAR SYSTEM EXPLORER
**Pawan Jha Mathematics Classes**

All planetary textures utilized in this application are derived from scientific observations and high-resolution spacecraft photography. They are hosted locally within the project to ensure offline reliability and deterministic loading performance.

---

### 1. Solar System Scope (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International — CC BY 4.0)
Source: [https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/)

The following 2K equirectangular maps are provided under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license:
- **Mercury**: `2k_mercury.jpg` (Messenger mission mosaic)
- **Venus Atmosphere**: `2k_venus_atmosphere.jpg` (Pioneer Venus & Akatsuki UV/VIS imagery)
- **Venus Surface**: `2k_venus_surface.jpg` (Magellan radar altimetry synthetic map)
- **Earth Daymap**: `2k_earth_daymap.jpg` (NASA Blue Marble Next Generation)
- **Earth Nightmap**: `2k_earth_nightmap.jpg` (Suomi NPP VIIRS Day/Night Band)
- **Earth Clouds**: `2k_earth_clouds.jpg` (Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer / MODIS cloud mosaic)
- **Moon**: `2k_moon.jpg` (Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter / LROC WAC mosaic)
- **Mars**: `2k_mars.jpg` (Mars Global Surveyor MOC & Viking orbiters)
- **Jupiter**: `2k_jupiter.jpg` (Cassini ISS cylindrical mosaic)
- **Saturn**: `2k_saturn.jpg` (Cassini ISS composite)
- **Saturn Rings**: `2k_saturn_ring_alpha.png` (Cassini ISS calibrated opacity & radial optical depth profile)
- **Uranus**: `2k_uranus.jpg` (Voyager 2 true-color calibrated cylindrical mosaic)
- **Neptune**: `2k_neptune.jpg` (Voyager 2 narrow-angle camera global mosaic)
- **Sun**: `2k_sun.jpg` (Solar Dynamics Observatory / SDO AIA 4500 Å continuum)

### 2. NASA / USGS / JHUAPL / SwRI (Public Domain)
- **Pluto**: `2k_pluto.jpg`
  - **Source**: NASA / Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory (JHUAPL) / Southwest Research Institute (SwRI) / USGS Astrogeology Science Center.
  - **Mission**: *New Horizons* spacecraft flyby (July 14, 2015).
  - **Instruments**: Long Range Reconnaissance Imager (LORRI) & Ralph Multispectral Visual Imaging Camera (MVIC).
  - **Coverage**: Global cylindrical projection featuring Sputnik Planitia ("The Heart of Pluto"), Cthulhu Macula, and Tombaugh Regio.
  - **Status**: Public Domain (NASA / US Government Work).

### 3. Auxiliary Data Maps (Specularity, Bump, Elevation)
- `earth_specular.jpg`: NASA ocean/water mask indicating 100% specular reflectance on oceans and 0% on continental landmasses.
- Surface Bump Maps (`mercury_bump.jpg`, `earth_bump.jpg`, `mars_bump.jpg`, `moon_bump.jpg`): Derived from USGS digital elevation models (DEM), LRO LOLA, and MGS MOLA laser altimetry.
